There seem to be two versions of the “Led & Key” TM1638 board. The first one I ordered has a light blue silkscreen and the designator U1 printed next to the chip, and J1 printed next to the pin connector strip. The ‘&’ in ‘Led & Key’ is also quite elegant, whereas on the boards that I got delivered later use a more stylized and bold font for the “Led & Key” phrase. These boards have small ‘+’ signs next to the LEDs and a darker blue silkscreen.

What’s a bit annoying is that the distances between the mounting holes in the corners of the circuit boards for these two models differ slightly. For this reason, I had to design the simple enclosure in two versions. You’ll have to figure out for yourself which one is best suited for your application.

On the bottom of the enclosure, there are four studs with a 4.4mm hole for M3x5 melt-in wire stubs to secure the board. These can be drilled out if the PCB is mounted with standard M3 screws.
